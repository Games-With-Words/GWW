Say Less.

You get a secret. Everyone else has to say it out loud — and the funniest wrong answer can still win the round.

  1. Get the secret.
    One player sees the answer, plus a short list of the words they're forbidden from using.
  2. Write the clue.
    Up to twenty words. Room to tell a story, land an in-joke, or bury a memory only your people would catch.
  3. Everyone guesses.
    Close counts. A typo or a plural still lands, because being right shouldn't hinge on spelling.
  4. Then the room votes.
    Every guess goes up anonymously and the room crowns two — funniest, and closest. A round nobody got still ends in a laugh, and a score.
  5. The reveal.
    Who wrote what, who won it both ways, and the line you'll all still be quoting next week.
